## 💡 What is Academy Notify?

**Academy Notify 2.0** is an enterprise-grade Windows desktop application created specifically for **private academies, tutoring centers (مراكز الدعم والدروس الخصوصية), training institutes, and educational centers**.

Running an educational academy requires tracking hundreds of students, managing multiple teachers, organizing weekly timetables across different rooms, monitoring tuition payments, and keeping parents updated.

**Academy Notify unifies all these operations into one modern, ultra-fast desktop platform.** 
It eliminates paper records and scattered spreadsheets, automates messaging via **Telegram** and **WhatsApp**, and works **100% offline** without risking downtime if your internet connection fails.

---

## 🎯 Who is it For?

Academy Notify is designed to empower every person involved in the academy:

* **🏫 Academy Directors & Owners (إدارة المركز)**:
  Monitor real-time academy operations, review student enrollments, inspect tuition revenue, track unpaid balances, and evaluate message delivery logs.
* **💼 Administrative Staff & Receptionists (الاستقبال والموظفون)**:
  Register new students in seconds, organize courses and study groups, manage weekly classroom timetables, plan pre-exam revision workshops, and send mass announcements.
* **👨‍🏫 Teachers & Instructors (الأساتذة)**:
  Keep track of assigned groups, access updated student rosters, coordinate pre-exam revision schedules, and stay aligned with academy management.
* **💵 Cashiers & Accountants (أمناء الصندوق والمحاسبون)**:
  Record student tuition payments (full, partial, or installments), track debts with automatic balance calculations, and instantly print branded official receipts.
* **👨‍👩‍👦 Parents & Students (الأولياء والتلاميذ)**:
  Receive instant automated alerts on their phones (via Telegram or WhatsApp) regarding class schedules, timetable changes, teacher absences, revision sessions, exam dates, and payment receipts.

## 🌟 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. 👥 Complete Student & Family Management
* **Rich Student Profiles**: Store student full names, phone numbers, grade levels, school streams, and Telegram IDs.
* **Multi-Child Family Linking**: Easily link a parent to multiple enrolled brothers and sisters.
* **Instant Search & Filters**: Search through thousands of students in milliseconds by name, phone number, enrolled course, or academic grade.

### 2. 🗓️ Courses, Groups & Weekly Timetable
* Supports both **Group Classes** (أفواج جماعية) and **Private 1-on-1 Sessions** (دروس فردية).
* **Interactive Weekly Schedule**: Color-coded calendar showing class hours, assigned teachers, and room numbers.
* **Conflict Prevention**: Built-in logic alerts you if a teacher or classroom is double-booked at the same time.

### 3. 📝 Exam Revision & Prep Sessions (حصص المراجعة والتحضير)
* Specialized tools to schedule and manage intensive workshops prior to official exams (BAC, BEM, finals).
* Set session dates, times, customized pricing, and targeted subjects.
* Send automated one-click invitations and reminders to all candidate students.

### 4. 📢 Automated Multi-Channel Notifications
* **Telegram Bot Integration**:
  * Send direct individual alerts or mass broadcasts by course, grade level, or revision group.
  * Interactive student self-registration: students open the bot, press `/start`, and link their profile.
  * Automatic parent verification.
* **WhatsApp Messaging**: Broadcast updates directly to parents' WhatsApp numbers.
* **Smart Dynamic Templates**: Build custom message templates with automatic placeholders:
  * `{{name}}` — Student or Parent name
  * `{{course}}` — Course / subject name
  * `{{date}}` — Date of class or revision session
  * `{{time}}` — Start time
  * `{{amount}}` — Paid amount or remaining tuition balance
* **Delivery Audit Logs**: Comprehensive history of every message sent, with instant status confirmation (Sent, Failed, Queued).

### 5. 💬 Built-in Live Chat Center
* Two-way messaging hub directly inside the desktop app.
* Respond to parent questions and student inquiries over Telegram without leaving the dashboard.

### 6. 💳 Cashier, Payments & Receipt Printing
* **Flexible Tuition Handling**: Log full payments, deposit advances, or partial installments.
* **Automatic Balance Tracking**: Know exactly which students have unpaid tuition at any moment.
* **Thermal POS & A4 Printing**:
  * Seamless support for **58mm and 80mm POS Thermal Receipt Printers**.
  * Support for **Standard A4 Office Printers** for formal monthly statements.
  * Branded printouts: academy logo, student name, course details, payment breakdown, date, and cashier signature.

### 7. 🛡️ Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
* Protect sensitive business data with 4 distinct user access levels:
  * **Admin**: Unrestricted access to all modules, financial figures, staff management, and system settings.
  * **Staff**: Student registration, course scheduling, and announcement dispatching.
  * **Teacher**: View timetables, student attendance rosters, and revision sessions.
  * **Cashier**: Focused exclusively on tuition payments, outstanding balances, and receipt printing.

### 8. ⚡ 100% Offline-First Reliability + Cloud Sync
* **Works Without Internet**: The local database engine ensures you can register students, record payments, and print receipts even during total internet blackouts.
* **Automatic Cloud Backup**: When an internet connection is available, data automatically synchronizes with the cloud to ensure your records are safely backed up.

### 9. 🌐 Native Bilingual Interface (Arabic RTL & English LTR)
* Full **Arabic** (العربية - Right to Left) and **English** (Left to Right) support.
* Instant one-click language toggle directly from the top navigation bar.

## 🚀 Quick Setup Guide

### Step 1: Download & Install
1. Head to the **[Releases](https://github.com/lamri-ala/academy-notify-2.0/releases/latest)** section.
2. Download `Academy.Notify.Setup.2.0.0.exe` (or the portable version).
3. Run the `.exe` file and follow the on-screen installation steps.

### Step 2: Configure Your Telegram Bot (5 Minutes)
1. Open Telegram on your phone or PC and search for **@BotFather**.
2. Type `/newbot` and follow the quick prompts to choose a name for your academy bot.
3. BotFather will provide an API Token (e.g., `123456789:ABCdef...`).
4. In Academy Notify, open **Settings (الإعدادات)**, paste your Bot Token, and click **Test Connection (فحص الاتصال)**.
5. Share your bot link with your students and parents!

### Step 3: Start Managing
* Add your teachers and classroom courses.
* Enroll students and print their registration slips.
* Schedule classes and send instant notifications!
